PieceWork · runs entirely in your browser

Run programs over your data, where it is stored

Your files live in a data set at a storage provider on Filecoin Calibration. Here you order work over them: the price is held in Filecoin Pay, the provider runs the program next to your files, writes the results back into your data set, and is paid only when it delivers. Nothing here runs on anyone's server — your wallet signs, and your browser talks to the provider and to the chain.

Not connected.

What this is

Buying compute from the storage provider that already holds your data, paid through Filecoin Pay. It runs on Filecoin Calibration, the public test network, so the USDFC and FIL here are test tokens from a faucet rather than money — everything else is the real thing: the contracts, your signatures, the provider, the proofs.
