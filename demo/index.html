Filecoin Onchain Cloud · proof of concept

Compute next to your data, paid through Filecoin Pay

Your files are kept by a storage provider on Filecoin. Now that same provider can also do work on your files, right where they are, and a bank-like contract (Filecoin Pay) makes sure everyone is paid fairly. Two ways to buy: rent a machine and pay for the time, like a taxi meter, or order a job and pay once when it is delivered.

ⓘThis is a simulation: no real money, no wallet needed. Follow the blue box on the left — it tells you what is happening and which button to press next. The money rules follow the real Filecoin Pay contract (source). Money is in USDFC, a dollar stablecoin.

For engineers: what happens on-chain at each click

Calls the proposed ComputeService contract would make, with the Filecoin Pay and PDP functions they use. Newest first.

For engineers — proposed protocol: ComputeService acts as the client's Filecoin Pay operator. Session: openSession → sessionReady → closeSession (terminates the rail) → settleRail. Batch job: postJob (escrow) → commitOutputs (pay, then terminate) or expire (refund). Correctness of results is the client's responsibility, as with commercial GPU clouds; timeouts bound what each side can lose.
