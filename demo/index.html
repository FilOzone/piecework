Prototype · Filecoin Onchain Cloud

PieceWorkCompute where your data already lives

Your files are stored on Filecoin by a storage provider, who proves every day that it still has them. With PieceWork the same provider can also work on your files right where they are — no downloading, no moving data — and you pay from your wallet through Filecoin Pay, a payment contract that enforces the rules for both sides.

What to expect

1 · Your files are already stored

You have a data set on Filecoin: a few files (pieces) kept by a storage provider and proven daily. You can see them on the right.

2 · Pick a service

The storage provider runs it on its own machines, right next to your files. Rent a service and pay for the time it runs, like a taxi meter — or order a one-off job and pay once, when the result is delivered.

3 · Follow the blue box

It tells you what is happening and highlights the next button. Time runs by itself while a service or a job is running (sped up: one minute takes about a second).

This is a simulation: no real money, no wallet to connect. The payment rules follow the real Filecoin Pay contract (source). Money is in USDFC, a US-dollar stablecoin.

What's recorded on Filecoin

Every step that moves money or changes your files leaves a public record on the Filecoin chain. Newest first; the small line shows the contract call behind it.

The payment contract checks that work is delivered on time and that nobody is charged beyond what was agreed. Like commercial GPU clouds, it does not check the quality of results. Prototype of a proposed protocol · the numbers follow Filecoin Pay's rules: pay-per-epoch payment streams, a safety margin (lockup), one-time payments and a 0.5% network fee.
